=====================================================================
  TAMIL READING STUDY - INSTRUCTIONS FOR PARTICIPANTS
=====================================================================

Thank you for helping with this study. You will read short Tamil
sentences on your computer while the webcam estimates where you are
looking. It takes about 40 minutes the first time (installation
included) and about 25 minutes after that.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR DETAILS (filled in by the researcher)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Participant code : ________   (example: P07)
  List number      : ________   (0 or 1)
  Send results to  : vijay4.aiml.rymec@gmail.com

Please read and sign the consent form from the researcher before you
start.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS RECORDED
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Your key presses and answers
  - Numbers describing where your eyes point on the screen
  - NO photos, NO video, NO audio are saved
  - Your name is not recorded; only your participant code

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU NEED
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer or laptop with a webcam
  - Internet connection (only for installation)
  - A quiet room, light on your face (no bright window behind you)
  - About 40 minutes without interruptions

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 - INSTALL (only once)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Save TamilReadingStudy.zip to your Desktop.

2. Right-click the zip file > "Extract All..." > "Extract".
   A folder named TamilReadingStudy opens.

3. Inside the folder, double-click  setup.bat
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - A black window opens and installs the software (about 5 minutes).

4. If the black window says "Python 3.12 was not found":
   a. Open https://www.python.org/downloads/release/python-31210/
   b. Scroll down, click "Windows installer (64-bit)" and run it.
   c. IMPORTANT: on the first screen tick
      "Add python.exe to PATH", then click "Install Now".
   d. When it finishes, double-click setup.bat again.

5. At the end, setup checks your computer. When it says
   "Sit in front of the webcam for 4 seconds", look at the screen.
   You should see:   All checks passed. You can run run_session.bat.

   If it shows a PROBLEM line, see "IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG" below.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 - DO THE READING SESSION
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Before you start:
  - Close Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Camera, or anything using the webcam.
  - Plug in your laptop charger.
  - Sit about an arm's length from the screen.
  - Put your elbows on the table and rest your chin on your hands.
    Keep your head in this position for the whole session.

1. In the TamilReadingStudy folder, double-click  run_session.bat

2. Type your participant code and press Enter.
   Type your list number and press Enter.

3. CALIBRATION (about 3 minutes)
   - A yellow dot appears. Look at it and press SPACE.
   - Keep looking at the dot until it jumps to a new place.
   - Move only your EYES, not your head. There are 25 dots.
   - At the end a red dot follows your gaze. Press Q to continue.

4. READING (about 20 minutes)
   - First, 9 dots appear one by one: just look at each dot.
     A results screen follows: press C to continue.
   - Before each sentence a small dot appears on the left:
     look at it and press SPACE.
   - The whole sentence appears at once (long sentences take 2 to 4
     lines). Read it normally, the way you usually read. When you
     have finished, press ENTER.
   - There are 100 sentences in 4 parts.
   - After some sentences a word is shown with the question
     "was this word in the sentence?":
     press Y for yes (ஆம்), N for no (இல்லை).
   - After each part there is a short break and a few more dots.

5. When it says "Finished. Thank you!", the window closes and a
   folder opens with your results file, named like:
        P07_results_20261101_103000.zip

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 - SEND YOUR RESULTS BACK
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Find the results file. It is in:
        TamilReadingStudy\results\
   (the folder opens automatically at the end of the session)

2. Email that ONE file as an attachment to:
        vijay4.aiml.rymec@gmail.com
   Subject:  Tamil reading study - <your participant code>

   (WhatsApp or Google Drive is also fine if the researcher agrees.)

3. That's all. You may delete the TamilReadingStudy folder afterwards.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Problem                              What to do
  -----------------------------------  ------------------------------
  "Webcam could not be opened"         Close Zoom/Teams/Camera apps,
                                       then try again.
  "Face and eyes detected in xx%"      Turn on a light facing you,
  (less than 90%)                      remove sunglasses, sit closer.
  "sentences do not fit on one line"   Settings > Display > Scale:
                                       choose 100% or 125%, try again.
  Calibration says face detection      Improve lighting, sit straight
  is unstable                          in front of the camera.
  You pressed a wrong key / want to    Press ESC. Your data so far is
  stop                                 kept; send the results file.
  Anything else                        Take a photo of the black
                                       window and send it to the
                                       researcher.

Thank you for your time!
